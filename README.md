 Parking Lot Management System – Introduction
A Parking Lot Management System is designed to manage vehicles entering and exiting a parking area.

It handles:
- Vehicle registration.
- Parking spot allocation.
- Ticket generation.
- Parking fee calculation.
- Payment processing.
- Spot release after successful payment.
Main objective: Allocate a suitable parking spot to a vehicle, calculate its parking charges, and make the spot available again after exit.
